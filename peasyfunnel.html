Key Takeaways

• From all visitors: only ~1.14% reach sold (~73% close rate post-booking is strong)
• Once registration started: ~54% reach valuation complete, ~6% book, ~4% sold
• Biggest leaks: initial entry-to-form (~73%), Unbox acceptance (~71%), logistics steps

Process has clear async pauses after valuation & after booking.
